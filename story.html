The Method · Since 2021

We started Halo because good actives were being wasted on bad formulas.

The premise

The science on the core skincare actives is settled. Niacinamide, retinoids, vitamin C, ceramides, the acids. What is not settled is whether the bottle in your hand holds enough of one to matter.

So we built a line on a single rule. One active per formula, at a concentration we disclose, tested every batch. Nothing hides in a blend, and nothing is there for the story on the label.

How a formula is made

“If we cannot print the number, we do not make the product.”
Step 01

Pick the active, set the dose

We start from the concentration the research supports, then formulate to hold it stable. The number comes first, the formula serves it.

Step 02

Keep the base quiet

The rest of the formula exists to deliver and stabilise the active. No secondary actives, no fragrance, nothing that muddies the read.

Step 03

Assay every batch

Before a run ships, an outside lab confirms the active is present at the stated strength and the formula is clean. The batch code maps to that report.

Step 04

Print what is in the bottle

The label names the active and the percentage, the box carries the batch. You get to reason about your own routine.
